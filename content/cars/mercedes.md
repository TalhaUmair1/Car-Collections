# Mercedes-Benz: The Best or Nothing

Mercedes-Benz is a German luxury vehicle, bus, coach, and truck manufacturer. The brand is known for its luxury vehicles, buses, coaches, and trucks. Mercedes-Benz is headquartered in Stuttgart, Baden-Württemberg, Germany.

## History

Mercedes-Benz can trace its origins to 1886 when Karl Benz patented the Benz Patent-Motorwagen, widely regarded as the first practical motorcar. The company name derives from a 1926 merger between Karl Benz's Benz & Cie. and Gottlieb Daimler's Daimler-Motoren-Gesellschaft. The name "Mercedes" comes from Mercedes Jellinek, daughter of dealer Emil Jellinek, who used this name to denote the new Daimler motor-racing cars in 1900.

## Innovation & Technology

Mercedes-Benz has pioneered numerous automotive technologies throughout its history:

- First application of disc brakes in passenger cars
- Anti-lock braking system (ABS)
- Electronic Stability Program (ESP)
- Airbags and PRE-SAFE safety systems
- BlueTEC diesel technology
- EQ electric vehicle platform
- MBUX infotainment system with AI capabilities

## Popular Models

- **A-Class**: Entry-level luxury compact car
- **C-Class**: Compact executive car
- **E-Class**: Executive car
- **S-Class**: Luxury flagship sedan
- **G-Class**: Off-road luxury SUV
- **GLA/GLC/GLS**: SUV lineup
- **AMG GT**: High-performance sports car
- **EQ Series**: Electric vehicle lineup

## Safety Leadership

Mercedes-Benz has been a pioneer in automotive safety, introducing innovations such as crumple zones, the safety cage concept, and numerous active safety systems. The company's commitment to safety has resulted in many of their vehicles earning top ratings in crash tests worldwide.
